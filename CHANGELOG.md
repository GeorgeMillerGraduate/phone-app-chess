# 0.2

- Removed last-move square colouring. Pieces are drawn independently of the selection paint, fixing unwanted transparency.
- Replaced the text strip with a numbered White/Black move table using piece PNGs and algebraic notation. Tap a move in analysis to review that position.
- Added a Home screen and Home navigation, with Android Back support.
- Replaced Quick/Balanced/Deep with a numeric 400–3000 difficulty rating. These labels are approximate; strength has not been calibrated against human Elo ratings.
- Added engine-versus-engine matches, separate White/Black ratings, pause/resume, saved moves, PGN sharing and analysis. Leaving the match or app pauses it.
- Corrected handling of an ongoing online room’s JSON-null result.
